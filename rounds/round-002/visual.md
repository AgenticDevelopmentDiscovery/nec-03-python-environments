## Strengths

- **Document & presentation.** Splitting the old dual-branch mental-model diagram into two linear units — `env-mental-model.svg` (spec → lockfile) and `env-mental-model-2.svg` (lockfile → environment) — is a genuine improvement over one figure trying to hold both. Each is now a clean three-box pipeline with one arrow relationship, and each `##` unit reads as a complete beat on its own.
- **All three registers.** `figures/pin-vs-lock-tree.svg` and `figures/isolation-before-after.svg` share a consistent visual grammar — green marks the resolved/pinned/isolated state, orange or red marks the unresolved/conflicting one — and `drift-chain.svg` extends the same color logic (green box for the correct diagnosis, red box for the wrong one). A reader who has seen one figure reads the color coding in the next without a legend, in the PDF, the slides, and the website alike.
- **Presentation.** The Demo unit's sidecar (`03-content.concepts.md`) records a decision to *not* add a fifth figure there, reasoning that the prose is already an ordered, runnable sequence and a step-flow diagram would restate it. That is the right call — a figure that repeats a numbered list as boxes is exactly the defect this seat penalizes, and the team caught it themselves.
- **Document.** The `venv`/`uv` figures (`venv-create.svg`, `uv-lockfile.svg`) now carry information that used to live in prose (e.g. "own interpreter, empty site-packages," "hashes + platform pins — rerun to update") and the prose was cut accordingly — these figures cannot be deleted without losing content, which is the right test for whether a figure earns its place.

## Weaknesses

- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § `uv`: fast installs and lockfiles — opens with "`uv` does the same three jobs faster." "The same three jobs" refers back to the `venv` unit two headings earlier. In the document this transition works; as a standalone slide or a website reader landing here from search, "the same three jobs" has no antecedent on the page.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § How it works: lockfile to environment — opens with "The lockfile isn't a second spec." This presupposes the reader just saw "spec" defined in the immediately preceding unit. Reasonable for a two-part sequence read in order (document, or slides viewed start to finish), but it fails the website test this seat is briefed to apply: a reader who lands mid-document does not know what "a second spec" is being contrasted against.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § Pitfalls — the third bullet ("Locking the environment and forgetting the random seed") is the only place the seeding/locking-independence claim appears, and it is the one core claim in `03-content.concepts.md`'s Claims list that never gets a figure (the interpreter/spec/lockfile relationship and the venv/`uv` parity claim both do). Buried as the last of three pitfall bullets, it is easy to read as a minor caveat rather than the second reproducibility axis the topic proposal names explicitly.
